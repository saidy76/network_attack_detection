STATISTICAL LIMITATION
------------------------------------------------------------
The baseline experiments were evaluated on the same fixed validation dataset. Step 18 provides aggregate metrics, per-class metrics, and confusion matrices, but does not retain sample-level prediction vectors required for paired prediction-level significance tests such as McNemar's test. Therefore, Step 19 reports descriptive comparisons, absolute differences, relative improvements, class-wise analysis, and Pareto-style comparisons rather than claiming statistical significance. A future repeated or cross-validation experiment can be used for formal statistical significance testing.
