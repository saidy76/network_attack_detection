FINAL METHODOLOGICAL DECISION

INTERPRETATION
The frozen CIC-IDS2018 model cannot be directly evaluated on UNSW-NB15 without an unsupported feature transformation or retraining. Therefore external accuracy/F1 is intentionally not fabricated.

WHY EXTERNAL PREDICTION WAS NOT PERFORMED
The frozen CIC-IDS2018 Random Forest expects the 30 selected CIC-IDS2018 features. UNSW-NB15 uses a different feature representation. Predicting UNSW samples by merely selecting similarly named or vaguely related features would change the meaning of the model inputs and invalidate the external evaluation.

RESEARCH CONSEQUENCE
UNSW-NB15 remains an important external dataset for generalization analysis, but direct evaluation of the frozen CIC-IDS2018 model is not methodologically supported under the current frozen feature pipeline.
